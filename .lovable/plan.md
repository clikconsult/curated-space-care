# LESBEST Premium Property-Care Website

## Overview
Build a complete, interconnected editorial website for LESBEST: six primary pages plus dedicated project and article detail pages. The experience will position cleaning as discreet, high-standard property care rather than a commodity service.

## Pages and content
- **Home** — cinematic interior hero, asymmetrical brand introduction, six-service editorial preview, standards statement, working before/after slider, portfolio montage, testimonial, journal preview, and final image-led quote callout.
- **Services** — six substantial alternating service chapters with inclusions, suitable-property guidance, supporting imagery, and quote actions.
- **About** — image-led brand story, philosophy, standards, people, commitment statement, and quote callout.
- **Portfolio** — filterable masonry-style project index for residential, commercial, deep-cleaning, and specialist work.
- **Project detail** — shareable project pages with large cover, narrative, services performed, before/after comparison, gallery, results, and quote action.
- **Blog** — magazine-style featured story, category filters, and a varied editorial article feed.
- **Article detail** — shareable reading pages with a narrow text measure, serif headings, pull quotes, related stories, and a closing quote action.
- **Contact** — calm quote form with all requested fields, placeholder contact details, service areas, and a restrained location treatment.

## Shared experience
- Minimal sticky navigation with responsive menu and **Request a Quote** action.
- Shared footer with navigation, services, placeholder contact details, social links, and the LESBEST closing statement.
- Exact palette: dark brown `#462E2C`, burnt orange `#D26638`, warm yellow `#F5E22E`, cream `#F9ECEB`, white, and near-black `#1E1414`.
- Instrument Serif for editorial display typography and Manrope for interface and body text.
- Semantic design tokens, thin rules, low corner radii, generous whitespace, controlled asymmetry, and no gradients, glass effects, decorative blobs, or generic card grids.
- Subtle reveal, image-scale, underline, menu, filter, and page interaction motion with reduced-motion support.
- Purpose-generated luxury interior photography focused on finished environments, natural light, warm material detail, premium residences, and executive workplaces.
- Purposeful mobile compositions rather than simple desktop stacking.

## Interactions and behavior
- Portfolio and journal category filters.
- Draggable and keyboard-accessible before/after comparison.
- Validated quote form with a polished confirmation state; submission remains local because no destination or inbox integration was supplied.
- Active navigation, accessible focus states, useful image alt text, and keyboard-friendly controls.

## Technical details
- Implement using the existing TanStack Start and Tailwind v4 structure.
- Create a shared site shell and focused reusable editorial components.
- Add route-specific metadata for every content page and detail route.
- Use representative LESBEST project and article copy; placeholder phone, WhatsApp, email, address, and service-area details will be clearly marked for later replacement.
- Verify the main paths and interactions at desktop and mobile sizes in the live preview.
